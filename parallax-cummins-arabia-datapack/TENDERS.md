### Dubai Metro Gold Line (42 km fully underground, 18 stations): design-build prequalification for civil, electromechanical, rolling stock and rail systems (station/depot emergency-power scope inside E&M)

**Buyer:** Roads & Transport Authority (RTA), Dubai · **Country:** United Arab Emirates · **Closes:** 2026-10-09 · **Estimate:** USD 5.0M–16.0M · **Lean:** maybe

**Estimate basis:** ESTIMATE anchored on a real benchmark: the original Dubai Metro used 20 Cat sets of 320-1,820 kW (mostly 3512/3516 class) for emergency power (Cat testimonial). Gold Line has 18 underground stations plus depot/OCC, all underground, so roughly 25-40 sets of 1-2 MW at USD 200-400k packaged = USD 5-16M equipment; low case assumes a DEWA dual-feed design with gensets mainly at depot/OCC and life-safety loads. Construction rental (TBM shafts, station boxes, 2028-2031) is extra and not included.

**Why this tier:** Estimated USD 4-15M of standby and rental power inside a USD 9.2bn metro line; the main contractor buys it in 2027-2029.

**Cummins products:** C2000D5 / C2500D5 / C3000D5 QSK60/QSK78 standby sets with PowerCommand paralleling, Centum Series standby sets, Rental fleet for tunnelling and station-box construction power · **Likely competitors:** Caterpillar via Al-Bahar (supplied 20 emergency sets to the original Dubai Metro), mtu / Rolls-Royce Power Systems, Perkins / FG Wilson and Chinese packagers (likely with Chinese-led consortia), Aggreko and Altaaqa (construction rental)

WHAT IT IS
Prequalification (RFQ) for the single design-and-build main contract of Dubai Metro's Gold Line (Metro Line 4), run by Dubai's Roads & Transport Authority (RTA). MEED (7 Sep 2026): "Dubai's Roads & Transport Authority (RTA) has set a deadline of 9 October for contractors to submit their prequalification statements for a contract to build the new Gold Line ... The previous deadline was 7 September." Cost about AED34bn ($9.2bn); completion September 2032. Cummins does not prequalify; the genset and rental opportunity sits with the winning consortium's E&M member or MEP subcontractor.

SCOPE & RATINGS
"A fully underground line, approximately 42 km in length. 18 underground stations" plus a depot (RTA EOI notice, Railway Gazette 25 Jun 2026); scope lists "tunnelling, underground civil works, station works (architectural, structural, MEP), rail systems, roads/utilities, safety/security systems ... and depot construction". Depth up to 40 m (Khaleej Times via Wikipedia). Genset scope: NOT itemised in any public source; no RTA genset standard found publicly.
